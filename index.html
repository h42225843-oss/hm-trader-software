About H.M Trader

Built for a real shop, by the shop that runs it.

H.M Trader started as a way to run H.M Traders' own bottle wholesale business without a paper ledger — tracking sales, stock, customers and dues that used to live across notebooks and memory.

It's now a complete shop ledger, kept simple on purpose, and built to work the way a small shop actually runs its day.
